Adds a customizable header overlay that displays at the start of the day.

[h2]Features[/h2]
Go to: Options > Mod options > "Dawn of a New Day";
[list]
[*][b]Enabled:[/b] You can toggle the overlay anytime without restarting.
[*][b]Screenshot Mode:[/b] When in Screenshot Mode (F11), if enabled the overlay won't be displayed.

[*][b]Show Example:[/b] Shows your current overlay configuration in the next tick to ease and familarize with the settings.
[/list]
[h3]Appearance Section[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Show Highlight:[/b] Adds a low-opacity light background behind the overlay.

[*][b]Scale:[/b] Scales the whole overlay to your like (From 0.5x to 4x).

[*][b]Offset Presets:[/b] Helps you to choose an offset of your liking without trial and error.
[*][b]Offset sliders (px):[/b] Choose offsets on X and Y axis at pixel-precision (From 0 To Screen Resolution).

[*][b]Line Width (%):[/b] Choose the width in percentage of the line that divides the Upper from Bottom (From 0 to 100).
[*][b]Line Thickness (px):[/b] Choose the thickness in pixels of the line that divides the Upper from Bottom (From 0 to 100)
[*][b]Line Padding (px):[/b] Choose the padding in pixels that divides the line from the Upper and Bottom (From 0 to 100)
[/list]
[h3]Duration Section[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Total Display Duration:[/b] Choose the total duration of the overlay in seconds (From 0 to 120).
[*][b]Fade In Duration:[/b] Choose the duration of the overlay fading-in in seconds (From 0 to 120).
[*][b]Fade Out Duration:[/b] Choose the duration of the overlay fading-out in seconds (From 0 to 120).
[/list]
[h3]Text Section[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Choose Font Family:[/b] Choose an custom OS font to replace the default game font of the text.
[*][b]Font Size:[/b] Choose the character size of the text (From 1 to 256).
[*][b]Outline Thickness:[/b] Choose the thickness of the outline of the text (From 0 to 10).
[*][b]Bold:[/b] Enable to add bold style to the text.
[*][b]Italic:[/b] Enable to add italic style to the text.
[*][b]Text Color:[/b] Choose from the color presets the color of the text.
[*][b]Outline Color:[/b] Choose from the color presets the color of the outline of the text.
[/list]
[h3]Label Format Section[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Upper Text Format:[/b] Choose the format of the Upper text.
[*][b]Bottom Text Format:[/b] Choose the format of the Bottom text.
[/list]

[tr]
	[th][b]Format[/b][/th]
	[th][b]Description[/b][/th]
[/tr]

[/tr]
[/table]
[h3]Extra Section[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Starts at Zero:[/b] Enable to start the count of the days from 0 instead of 1.
[*][b]Show Every X Days:[/b] Choose how often the overlay displays (From 1 to 1200) [eg. X=10: DAY 10, DAY 20, DAY 30, ...].
[*][b]Trigger Hour:[/b] Choose at what hour of the day the overlay displays (From 0 to 23).
[*][b]Day Relative:[/b] Choose what the value of day will be relative to.
[/list]
[table]
[tr]
	[th][b]Name[/b][/th]
	[th][b]Description[/b][/th]
[/tr]
[tr]
	[th]Settle[/th]
	[th]Day will be relative to the day the colonists settled.[/th]
[/tr]
[tr]
	[th]Quadrum[/th]
	[th]Day will be relative to the quadrum resetting itself after 15 days.[/th]
[/tr]
[tr]
	[th]Season[/th]
	[th]Day will be relative to the season resetting itself after it ends.[/th]
[/tr]
[tr]
	[th]Year[/th]
	[th]Day will be relative to the year resetting itself after 60 days.[/th]
[/tr]
[/table]
