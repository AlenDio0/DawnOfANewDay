[h1]FEATURES[/h1]
Go to: Options > Mod options > "Dawn of a New Day";
[h3]Header/General[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Enabled:[/b] You can toggle the overlay anytime without restarting.
[*][b]Screenshot Mode:[/b] When in Screenshot Mode (F11), if enabled the overlay won't be displayed.
[*][b]Show Example:[/b] Shows your current overlay configuration in the next tick to ease and familarize with the settings.
[/list]
[h3]Appearance[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Show Highlight:[/b] Adds a low-opacity light background behind the overlay.
[*][b]Scale:[/b] Scales the whole overlay to your like.
[*][b]Offset Presets:[/b] Helps you to choose an offset of your liking without trial and error.
[*][b]Offset sliders (px):[/b] Choose offsets on X and Y axis at pixel-precision.
[*][b]Line Width (%):[/b] Choose the width in percentage of the line that divides the Upper from Bottom.
[*][b]Line Thickness (px):[/b] Choose the thickness in pixels of the line that divides the Upper from Bottom.
[*][b]Line Padding (px):[/b] Choose the padding in pixels that divides the line from the Upper and Bottom.
[/list]
[h3]Duration[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Total Display Duration:[/b] Choose the total duration of the overlay in seconds.
[*][b]Fade In Duration:[/b] Choose the duration of the overlay fading-in in seconds.
[*][b]Fade Out Duration:[/b] Choose the duration of the overlay fading-out in seconds.
[/list]
[h3]Text[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Choose Font Family:[/b] Choose an custom OS font to replace the default game font of the text.
[*][b]Font Size:[/b] Choose the character size of the text.
[*][b]Outline Thickness:[/b] Choose the thickness of the outline of the text.
[*][b]Bold:[/b] Enable to add bold style to the text.
[*][b]Italic:[/b] Enable to add italic style to the text.
[*][b]Text Color:[/b] Choose from the color presets the color of the text.
[*][b]Outline Color:[/b] Choose from the color presets the color of the outline of the text.
[/list]
[h3]Label Format[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Upper Text Format:[/b] Choose the format of the Upper text.
[*][b]Bottom Text Format:[/b] Choose the format of the Bottom text.
[/list]
[table]
[tr]
	[th][b]Format[/b][/th]
	[th][b]Description[/b][/th]
[/tr]
[tr]
	[th]{D/d}[/th]
	[th]Full Day[/th]
[/tr]
[tr]
	[th]{Y/y}[/th]
	[th]Full Year [eg. 5500] / 2-Digit Year [eg. 00][/th]
[/tr]
[tr]
	[th]{Q/q}[/th]
	[th]CAPS Quadrum / Quadrum[/th]
[/tr]
[tr]
	[th]{S/s}[/th]
	[th]CAPS Season / Season[/th]
[/tr]
[tr]
	[th]{H/h}[/th]
	[th]Full Hour [eg. 06] / Hour [eg. 6][/th]
[/tr]
[/table]
[h3]Sound[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Enabled:[/b] Toggle the sound that starts with the overlay.
[*][b]Choose Sound:[/b] Choose a custom sound from the catalogue.
[*][b]Sound Volume:[/b] Choose the volume of the sound.
[*][b]Pitch Volume:[/b] Choose the pitch of the sound.
[/list]
[h3]Extra[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Starts at Zero:[/b] Enable to start the count of the days from 0 instead of 1.
[*][b]Show Every X Days:[/b] Choose how often the overlay displays [eg. X=10: DAY 10, DAY 20, DAY 30, ...].
[*][b]Trigger Hour:[/b] Choose at what hour of the day the overlay displays.
[*][b]Day Relative:[/b] Choose what the value of day will be relative to.
[/list]
[table]
[tr]
	[th][b]Name[/b][/th]
	[th][b]Description[/b][/th]
[/tr]
[tr]
	[th]Settle[/th]
	[th]Day will be relative to the day the colonists settled.[/th]
[/tr]
[tr]
	[th]Quadrum[/th]

[/tr]

	[th]Season[/th]
	[th]Day will be relative to the season resetting itself after it ends.[/th]
[/tr]

	[th]Year[/th]

[/tr]
[/table]

[b]If you want to integrate a translation for the mod, follow these steps:[/b]
[olist]
[*]Go into the GitHub Repository ([url=https://github.com/AlenDio0/DawnOfANewDay]DawnOfANewDay[/url])
[*]Clone the repository in your device.
[*]Look for "Languages" folder.
[*]Copy the path from "English" and rename it into your language.
[*]Go into "<YourLanguage>\Keyed.Misc.xml" and translate the file in your own language.
[*]Finally, commit your changes and create a Pull Request into the main branch of the repository.
[/olist]
